About Field-Fox

Better decisions begin with a clearer view.

Field-Fox is exploring how thoughtful technology can turn complex field data into useful, timely guidance for the people who grow our food and care for the land.

Our direction

Practical intelligence, built around real field work.

Agricultural data is most valuable when it supports a decision. Field-Fox aims to bring sensor readings, observations, and activity into one calm, understandable experience.

The goal is simple: reduce guesswork, make important changes easier to spot, and help growers spend more time acting on information than organizing it.
